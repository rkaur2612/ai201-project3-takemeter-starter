analysis: An argument where a specific, checkable fact (a film, a date, a figure, a quote) carries the point rather than decorating it.
hot_take: A confident opinion or claim with no checkable support; strength of feeling or personal taste is not support.
reaction: A quick emotional or humorous response to something just seen or read; feeling or a joke rather than an argument.
question: A post whose main purpose is to ask something or request recommendations, even if it opens with an opinion.
